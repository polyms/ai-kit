# Business decision

**Use when:** The user requests evidence, financial or strategic analysis, critique and a stakeholder
decision message. Read the composition protocol.

| Stage | Input / output | Run when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| research | Question and scope -> dated evidence | Current sources or comparative evidence are needed. |
| business | Evidence, objectives and constraints -> decision analysis | Options, economics or feasibility need evaluation. |
| review | Analysis -> material findings | Independent critique or assumption stress-testing is requested. |
| comms | Decision status and rationale -> message draft | A stakeholder response is requested. |

**Gates:** Missing material costs or weak evidence must stay visible. Use bounded scenarios if
useful, not precise invented values. If review invalidates a central premise, return to business
for a permitted revision, then recheck. Stop if a stakeholder choice is required. Recommended
pricing is not approved pricing. Carry assumptions, version, units and decision status forward;
honor the requested level of detail without converting uncertainty into a commitment.

**Finish:** Return the requested analysis and communication, explicitly proposed or confirmed. Do
not change commercial settings, publish records, send messages or announce approval without
authorization.

# Business analysis

Use [business-case-template.md](business-case-template.md) and the shared work artifact standard
(`docs/agents/work-artifact-standard.md`). For explicit business requirements, use `reqs`.

For pricing, strategy or decision records, use [decision-templates.md](decision-templates.md).

1. Define the decision, stakeholders, objectives and constraints.
2. Gather relevant evidence. Use research tools where available and distinguish sourced facts from
   estimates and assumptions. State the source date, currency, units and time horizon.
3. Compare viable options against the same criteria, including the status quo where relevant.
4. For quantitative work, make calculations auditable; state inputs and formulas, test sensitivity and
   avoid unsupported precision. Do not invent demand, revenue, costs or market size.
5. Recommend an option with rationale, trade-offs, risks and conditions for revisiting the decision.
6. Write the case using the template and the user's desired level of detail; identify open decisions.

For a material recommendation, challenge the highest-impact assumption and record the validation or
revisit trigger. Hand off to `review` when independent scrutiny is requested.

Draft by default. Do not treat a recommendation as approved or modify product pricing without authorization.

## Future imports

Check the license of the exact revision and file, including referenced assets, dependencies and
third-party notices. Public availability is not a license. If importing or adapting MIT expression,
preserve copyright and license text; for Apache-2.0, preserve required licenses and notices, mark
modified files, and review attribution obligations. Do not relicense restricted or share-alike content
as MIT. For noncommercial, unclear or source-available terms, avoid redistribution without explicit
permission or legal review. Keep a file-level provenance record for any actual imported material.

These observations support engineering decisions, not a legal opinion. Recheck licenses before import.

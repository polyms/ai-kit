## Pricing and unit economics

State currency, billing period, usage unit, cost assumptions and source dates.
Use one consistent treatment of taxes, discounts, platform fees and fixed/variable costs.

| Plan / scenario | Price | Included usage | Estimated variable cost | Contribution | Margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Name] | [Value] | [Units] | [Value or range] | [Value] | [% or N/A] |

Contribution = net revenue minus attributable variable cost.
Contribution margin = contribution / net revenue when net revenue is positive.
Minimum price at a target margin = variable cost / (1 - target margin), before adjustments
for fees, taxes or discounts not already included. Define gross versus contribution margin
and do not equate them when the underlying cost definitions differ.

Compare conservative, expected and high-usage cases using the same assumptions.
Flag sensitivity, unsupported inputs, rounding and margin impact. Use user-specified
presentation detail without hiding material uncertainty.

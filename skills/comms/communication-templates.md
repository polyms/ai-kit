# Communication templates

These are composition guides, not mandatory headings. For chat and ticket comments, use natural prose
and omit labels unless useful. Do not insert placeholders in a send-ready message.

## Client response

[Acknowledge or answer the specific question.]
[Explain relevant facts and trade-offs briefly.]
[State the agreed next action, or ask the necessary question.]

## Ticket decision comment

[Decision or proposal, clearly distinguished.]
[Brief rationale and scope/constraints.]
[Next action and owner/date only when confirmed.]

## Progress update

[What is complete and verified.]
[Current work / blocker and impact.]
[Next step; schedule only if supported.]

## Escalation

[Issue and business impact.]
[Evidence, current mitigation and unresolved risk.]
[Decision or assistance requested; required timing only when established.]

## Announcement / email

Subject: [Specific purpose or change.]
[Why the reader is receiving this.]
[What changes and who is affected.]
[Required action, effective date and help channel when known.]

## Bilingual response

Provide complete versions under clear language labels. Keep decisions, numbers, dates and
technical terms equivalent; do not add claims in either version.

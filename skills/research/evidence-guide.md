# Evidence and synthesis guide

Use for consequential research, source audits, competitive analysis or recurring digests.

## Claim-level verification

| Claim | Source / date | Evidence type | Confidence / limitations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Specific assertion] | [Actual URL or workspace reference] | [Primary / secondary / estimate] | [Reason] |

Check that the source supports the exact claim, not merely the same topic. Prefer original evidence
or authoritative documentation. For material disputed claims, seek independent corroboration or explain
why it is unavailable. Distinguish event dates from publication dates; do not treat copied sources as
independent evidence. Identify stale pricing and version-sensitive information.

## Comparison

Use comparable criteria, units, date windows and denominators. Mark unavailable values; do not invent
rankings, normalize incompatible data silently, or equate marketing claims with verified results.

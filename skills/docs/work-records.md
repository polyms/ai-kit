# Work record templates

## Meeting notes

- Meeting and date: [Confirmed]
- Purpose: [Topic]
- Decisions: [Confirmed outcomes, not suggestions]
- Open issues: [Pending questions]
- Actions: [Action, owner/date only if agreed]
- References: [Real source links]

## Handover / knowledge capture

- Purpose and scope: [What the next session needs to know]
- Current state: [Verified status, branch/version when relevant]
- Established decisions and rationale: [Source or record link]
- Key artifacts: [Actual paths or URLs]
- Remaining work and blockers: [Actionable next steps]
- Verification: [What was tested, what was not]

For internal knowledge capture, distinguish durable guidance from project-specific context. Redact
secrets and sensitive data. Do not publish to a repository or workspace without authorization.

## Data analysis brief

- Question, data source, scope and snapshot date
- Definitions, metrics, denominators and missing data
- Findings: [Calculations, patterns and limitations]
- Interpretation: [Do not infer causality from correlation]
- Recommended actions or further validation

Use the smallest necessary format; attach a reproducible calculation or query when available and useful.
